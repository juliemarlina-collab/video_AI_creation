4 Main Processes

Each phase has its own vibrant tab. Click the cards below to switch content.

Phase 1 — Master Prompting & Video AI Overview

This is the planning phase. Lecturers decide the topic, objective, audience, learning value, characters, style and scene structure before creating anything visual.

  • Determine title / topic
  • Set learning objective and target audience
  • Define character consistency
  • Choose visual style and mood
  • Create 20-scene storyboard overview
  • Prepare master prompt for NotebookLM / Google Vids
Output: Master Prompt + Storyboard Plan

Teaching Direction

Topic, objective, moral value, audience and duration.

Story Design

Conflict, turning point, resolution and lesson.

Character Profile

Bawang Putih, Bawang Merah, Ibu Tiri, Nenek Tua, Jejaka Moden.

Storyboard Map

20 scenes × 9 seconds = 3 minutes.

Phase 2 — Scene-by-Scene Image Generation

Now each scene from the storyboard is turned into a visual image. This phase builds the visual foundation for the final video.

  • Use prompts generated in Phase 1
  • Generate each scene image in 16:9
  • Keep character face, outfit and mood consistent
  • Refine setting, emotion and atmosphere
  • Build a complete scene image set
Output: Full Scene Image Set

Scene Prompt

Still-image prompt for each scene.

Consistency Check

Outfit, background, expression and visual continuity.

Sample Output

Kampung scene, sungai scene, magical box, city café.

Lecturer Tip

Generate in sequence for easier continuity.

Phase 3 — Video AI Generation & Motion

This is the motion phase. The images are transformed into video clips using camera angle, movement, transitions and cinematic flow.

  • Convert scene images into moving clips
  • Add slow push-in, tracking shot, close-up and reveal
  • Use suitable transition from scene to scene
  • Ensure the sequence flows smoothly
  • Produce a silent draft video
Output: Silent Video Draft

Camera Language

Wide shot, close-up, POV, orbit shot, slow motion.

Transitions

Fade in, dissolve, magical glow, portal swirl, dramatic cut.

Sequence Flow

Keep the emotional arc clear from opening to ending.

Sample Motion

Scene 15: coffee hand placement → eye contact slow motion.

Phase 4 — Audio Enhancement & Finalization

This final phase completes the project with voice-over, music and sound support so the video becomes presentation-ready.

  • Add narration for each 9-second scene
  • Insert suitable background music
  • Add optional sound effects
  • Balance voice and music levels
  • Export the final video
Output: Final Complete Video

Voice-over

Short narration, clear BM, matches visual duration.

Music Mood

Village calm, magic reveal, café romance, conflict tension, hopeful ending.

Audio Sync

Match narration timing with the visual story beat.

Final Check

Export, subtitle, title card and shareable link test.

Scene 15 — Bertemu Jejaka Moden

Time: 2:06–2:15

Visual: Detik romantik di kafe. Seorang jejaka tampan memakai blazer memandang Bawang Putih dengan baik hati ketika dia menghidangkan kopi.

Video shooting prompt: Bawang Putih menghidangkan secawan kopi kepada seorang jejaka tampan. Ketika cawan diletakkan, tangan mereka hampir bersentuhan dan mata mereka bertemu. Gunakan close-up pandangan mata, slow motion halus dan soft focus background.

Transition: Slow motion transition daripada cawan kopi kepada eye contact.

Voice-over: “Di situlah dia bertemu seorang jejaka baik hati yang menghargai kelembutan dirinya.”

Music: Piano romantik lembut, strings halus dan suasana manis berdebar.
